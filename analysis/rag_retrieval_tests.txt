RAG RETRIEVAL TESTS (Owner: Yashasvi)
=====================================

Setup
-----
- Policies: 8 synthetic policies (all synthetic=true)
    * 1 Medicare LCD-style policy (lumbar MRI, jurisdiction JK)
    * 7 commercial policies for fictional payer "SampleCommercial" (jurisdiction national)
- Chunks: 46 (split at each policy's section headings)
- Embeddings: sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384-dim)
- Vector index: FAISS IndexFlatIP (cosine similarity on normalized vectors)
- Reranking: cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 (on by default)
- Filters: payer, procedure code, jurisdiction (applied in code after ranking)
- chunk_id format: policy_id::section_slug (cited by config/criteria_rules.yaml)

Note on scores: with reranking on, scores are raw cross-encoder relevance scores,
not percentages. They can be negative. Only the order matters.


TEST 1: Medicare policy, procedure 72148 (lumbar MRI)
------------------------------------------------------
Command:
  python src/rag/rag_pipeline.py demo   (query 1: payer=Medicare, procedure=72148)
Query: "How many weeks of conservative treatment are needed before a lumbar MRI?"

  #1  score=6.1589  medicare_lcd_lumbar_mri::coverage_indications_limitations_and_or_medical_necessity
      section=Coverage Indications, Limitations, and/or Medical Necessity
  #2  score=4.6441  medicare_lcd_lumbar_mri::red_flag_conditions
      section=Red Flag Conditions

Result: PASS. The correct section (6 weeks of conservative management) is #1,
and the red-flag exception is #2.


TEST 2: SampleCommercial policy, procedure 72148
------------------------------------------------
Command:
  python src/rag/rag_pipeline.py demo   (query 3: payer=SampleCommercial, procedure=72148)
Query: "What records must be sent with the prior authorization request?"

  #1  score=-1.2836   commercial_lumbar_mri::precertification_requirements
  #2  score=-10.9774  commercial_lumbar_mri::documentation_requirements

Result: PARTIAL. The correct section (Documentation Requirements) is #2, not #1.
This is the one question the reranker made worse: the phrase "prior authorization
request" matches the Precertification section's topic. The correct answer is
still in the top 3, which is why the agent reads the top 3 results.

TEST 5: Method comparison (22 questions: 8 easy, 14 hard)
---------------------------------------------------------
Command:
  python src/rag/rag_pipeline.py compare

"Easy" questions reuse words from the policy text.
"Hard" questions are reworded the way a clinician or staff member would ask.

Method                       Section names   Easy @1  Easy @3  Hard @1  Hard @3   All @1   All @3
Keyword (TF-IDF)             yes                 7/8      8/8     7/14    12/14    14/22    20/22
Keyword (TF-IDF)             no                  7/8      8/8     5/14    12/14    12/22    20/22
Embeddings (FAISS)           yes                 5/8      8/8    10/14    13/14    15/22    21/22
Embeddings (FAISS)           no                  4/8      8/8     8/14    13/14    12/22    21/22
Hybrid (both)                yes                 7/8      7/8     9/14    13/14    16/22    20/22
Hybrid (both)                no                  7/8      7/8     9/14    13/14    16/22    20/22
Embeddings (FAISS) + rerank  yes                 7/8      8/8    11/14    13/14    18/22    21/22
Hybrid (both) + rerank       yes                 7/8      8/8    11/14    13/14    18/22    21/22

Findings:
- Keyword search is best when the question reuses the policy's wording;
  embeddings are best on reworded questions.
- Embeddings + rerank is best overall: 18/22 at #1, 21/22 in top 3.
- Compared with plain embeddings, reranking fixed 4 questions and broke 1.
- Section names help embeddings once policies share a common layout (15 vs 12).

Still missed at #1 by the best method (4):
  [easy] minimum duration of physical therapy or medication before imaging
  [hard] What records must be sent with the prior authorization request?
  [hard] She drops things and walks unsteadily, does she have to finish six weeks
         of therapy before a neck scan?
  [hard] What paperwork does the surgeon need to send for a knee replacement?


LIMITATIONS
-----------
- Small test set (22 questions), written by the pipeline's author, so results are
  directions, not proof. Next step: questions written blind by teammates.
- All policies are synthetic. Real payer policies are under an AMA CPT license.
- FAISS has no built-in metadata filtering; filters are applied after ranking.
