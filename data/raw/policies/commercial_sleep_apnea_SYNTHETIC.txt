Clinical Policy Bulletin SC-CPB-007: Obstructive Sleep Apnea Testing and CPAP
Effective date: 01/01/2026   Next review: 01/01/2027
Scope: Fully insured commercial HMO, PPO, and POS members aged 18 and older.

Precertification Requirements
Precertification is required for attended in-lab sleep studies and for CPAP
devices. Home sleep apnea tests do not require precertification when ordered
for an adult who meets the criteria in this policy.

Medical Necessity Criteria
A home sleep apnea test is the preferred first test for adults with symptoms
of moderate to severe sleep apnea, such as loud snoring, witnessed pauses in
breathing, or daytime sleepiness. An attended in-lab study is medically
necessary when the member has significant heart or lung disease, a
neuromuscular condition, a suspected sleep disorder other than obstructive
apnea, a negative or inconclusive home test despite strong symptoms, or cannot
complete a home test.
CPAP is medically necessary when testing shows an apnea-hypopnea index of 15
or more, or 5 to 14 with sleepiness, high blood pressure, heart disease, or
prior stroke. Coverage continues after the first 90 days only if the device
download shows use of at least 4 hours per night on 70 percent of nights
during a 30-day period.

Not Medically Necessary
Sleep testing is not medically necessary for snoring alone without other
symptoms, or for repeat testing without a change in symptoms, weight, or
treatment.

Experimental, Investigational, or Unproven
Acoustic pharyngometry, and consumer wearables or smartphone applications used
as the only diagnostic test, are considered experimental and investigational.

Documentation Requirements
Submit the Epworth Sleepiness Scale score, symptom history, relevant medical
conditions, the sleep study report with the apnea-hypopnea index, and for
continued CPAP coverage the device usage download.

Background
Untreated obstructive sleep apnea is linked to daytime sleepiness, motor
vehicle crashes, and cardiovascular disease. CPAP works only when it is used
regularly, which is why continued coverage depends on usage data.

Codes
