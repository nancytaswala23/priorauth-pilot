Clinical Policy Bulletin SC-CPB-004: Brain MRI
Effective date: 01/01/2026   Next review: 01/01/2027
Scope: Fully insured commercial HMO, PPO, and POS members.

Precertification Requirements
Precertification is required for outpatient brain MRI with or without
contrast. Imaging ordered from the emergency department for suspected stroke
or acute neurologic change does not require precertification.

Medical Necessity Criteria
The plan considers brain MRI medically necessary when any one of the following
applies:
1. Headache with warning features: new headache after age 50, a significant
   change in an established headache pattern, headache that worsens with
   coughing or straining, headache in a member with cancer or a weakened
   immune system, or headache with an abnormal neurologic examination.
2. New-onset seizure.
3. New focal neurologic deficits, such as weakness, numbness, or vision loss.
4. Suspected or established demyelinating disease, including follow-up to
   guide treatment.
5. Monitoring of a known brain tumor, or pre-operative planning.

Not Medically Necessary
Brain MRI is not medically necessary for stable, long-standing migraine or
tension-type headache with a normal neurologic examination, for simple
fainting without neurologic signs, or as screening in members without
symptoms.

Experimental, Investigational, or Unproven
Functional MRI used to diagnose psychiatric conditions, and brain imaging used
for lie detection, are considered experimental and investigational.

Documentation Requirements
Submit headache history including onset, pattern, and any change, the
neurologic examination, any warning features, a description of seizure events
if applicable, and reports of prior brain imaging.

Background
Most primary headaches can be diagnosed from history and examination. Imaging
is reserved for warning features, because routine imaging finds incidental
abnormalities more often than causes of headache.

Codes
Procedure codes: 70551 (brain MRI without contrast); 70553 (brain MRI
without and with contrast)
