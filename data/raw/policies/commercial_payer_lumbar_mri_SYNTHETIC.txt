Advanced Imaging: Lumbar Spine MRI (CPT 72148)

Prior Authorization Requirements
Prior authorization is required for outpatient lumbar spine MRI. The ordering
provider must submit the request before the service is performed. Requests
submitted after the service date will be reviewed as retrospective requests
and may be denied for lack of authorization.

Coverage Criteria
Lumbar spine MRI is considered medically necessary when one of the following
is met:
1. Low back pain with or without radiculopathy persisting after a minimum of
   6 weeks of provider-directed conservative treatment within the past 6
   months, including at least one of physical therapy, chiropractic care, or
   prescribed medication, and the member is a candidate for surgery or
   interventional treatment.
2. A red flag condition is present: suspected cauda equina syndrome,
   progressive neurologic deficit, suspected infection, suspected malignancy,
   or suspected fracture.
3. Pre-operative planning for a scheduled lumbar spine surgery.

Exclusions
Lumbar MRI is not medically necessary for acute low back pain under 6 weeks
without red flag conditions, for screening, or when the results would not
change management.

Documentation Requirements
Submit clinical notes from the last 60 days documenting symptom onset and
duration, physical and neurologic exam findings, type and dates of
conservative treatment and the member's response, and any red flag findings.
